Frequently Asked Questions

What's your typical project timeline?

Simple websites typically take 2-4 weeks, while complex projects can take 2-6 months. The timeline depends on project scope, requirements, and client feedback cycles.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes, I offer maintenance packages to keep your website updated, secure, and running smoothly. This includes security updates, content changes, and performance optimization.

What technologies do you work with?

I specialize in modern frontend technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Vue.js, and responsive design frameworks. For backend, I work with Node.js, Express, and various databases.

How do we communicate during the project?

We typically use email, video calls (Zoom/Teams), and project management tools like Trello or Notion. I provide regular updates and welcome client feedback throughout the development process.
